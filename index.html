Welcome to the Cellar

"Step down off Wallgate, leave the streetlamps behind, and enter a candlelit sanctuary for raw expression."

The Click is an underground poetry lounge hosted in the legendary basement venue of The Boulevard, Wigan. Dedicated to spoken word, beat verse, unvarnished drafts, and rhythmic storytelling, every session offers an intimate stage for both seasoned voices and first-time writers.

Venue Location

The Boulevard, 29 Wallgate, Wigan WN1 1LD

Open Mic Nights

Every Tuesday evening from 7:30 PM. Grab a slot in advance or sign up at the door.

The Atmosphere

Low ceilings, dim amber light, cheap drinks, and a community that listens deeply.
