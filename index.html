Welcome to the Cellar

"Step down off Wallgate, leave the streetlamps behind, and enter a candlelit sanctuary for raw expression."

The Click is an underground poetry lounge hosted in the legendary basement venue of The Boulevard, Wigan. Dedicated to spoken word, beat verse, unvarnished drafts, and rhythmic storytelling, every session offers an intimate stage for both seasoned voices and first-time writers.

Venue Location

The Boulevard, 19 Wallgate, Wigan WN1 1LD

Whether you come to share your notebook, listen with a pint or an espresso, or simply collaborate with fellow writers in Wigan, you've found your place.

🕯️ Candlelit Open Mic

5-minute open slots for anyone with a story, rhyme, or monologue. Sign-ups begin 30 minutes before doors open.

🎷 Ambient Soundscapes

Immersive background acoustics, improvisational double bass, and soft soundscapes accompany every performance night.

📜 Safe Haven Rules

Zero pretension, full respect. We listen in quiet attention and support every artist who takes the mic.

Midnight in the Cellar

by Amy Rose

Smoke in the dim light, wax dripping low, we trade our clichés, for somewhere to go. The brick holds the echoes, the floorboards agree, we are all just writers, trying to be free.
